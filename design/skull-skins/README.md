# DeckHand skull skins: ten concepts

All artwork is original and drawn by script (`NN-name/make.py`, shared helpers in `lib.py`, standard library only; rendered with headless Chrome). Every mockup is a skull silhouette on a **transparent canvas** (`mockup-transparent.png`, `mockup.svg`); `mockup.png` shows it over a desktop-like wallpaper so the transparency reads. The track browser (and other large modules where it helps) are separate floating windows with their own shapes and a grip handle. `contact-sheet.png` has all ten; `index.html` is the gallery with the comparison table.

## 01 Anatomical Ivory
A restrained, anatomically literal ivory skull: each eye is a jog ring around a dark socket, and one continuous waveform band runs through both sockets and across the nasal bridge with a playhead at each pupil. Controls sit on dark engraved plates so nothing competes with the bone. Control families: Eye sockets: Jog ring (marker; Forehead / cranium: Title; Cheekbones: Play / cue / sync; Ears: Tempo fader + range; Nasal bridge / cavity: Mic on / talk / level; master. Floating: Ribcage browser (rib-cage silhouette with grip) and a status pill float below the spine. Usability 4/5 (plain labels, every region has one job).

## 02 Sugar Skull
A sugar skull whose eyes are flower rosettes that spin as the jog while each holds its own waveform lane; every knob is a marigold flower. Teeth are real buttons (both rows), pads move to the cheeks, and a strand of beat beads links the eyes. Control families: Eye rosettes: Jog (petal ring + marker); Forehead: Info; Crown flowers: 8 sampler pads; Ears: FX rows (3 slots) + tempo fader and range; Cheekbones: 8 hot-cue pads per deck (rounded). Floating: Browser on an ofrenda panel strung with papel-picado bunting; status pill. Usability 3/5 (ornament competes with labels).

## 03 Neon Chrome
A low-poly chrome skull with slanted holographic visors for eyes: the two decks share one band of stacked lanes (A on top, B below) that scrolls through both visors with a single playhead at the nasal bridge. Jog platters are HUD discs on the cheekbones; the ears are cyber headphone cups full of controls. Control families: Visors: Stacked waveform lanes A / B; Forehead: Info; Cheekbones: Jog HUD disc; Headphone ears: FX rows; Nose: Mic on / talk / level. Floating: Terminal browser (chamfered window), REC, settings / skin / language strip (small separate window). Usability 4/5 (high contrast, clear lanes).

## 04 Pixel Art
A chunky pixel skull on a 20 px grid whose sockets are small arcade screens: waveform lane above, a stepped-disc platter below. The jaw is long so the mixer fits as two vertical strips, like a classic channel pair. Control families: Eye sockets: Waveform lane + jog platter (pixel disc; Forehead: Info; Cheekbones: Transport; Ears: FX rows; Nose: Master section. Floating: Save-file browser and an arcade corner (REC, settings / skin / language). Usability 3/5 (big and clear, long jaw makes mixer far from decks).

## 05 Pirate Scraps
A bandana-wearing skull in brass portholes with crossed bones underneath: the bones are the mixer, with EQ knobs strung along each bone and a brass cap that slides diagonally for the channel fader. Deck controls live on four torn sailcloth scraps that float beside the skull. Control families: Portholes (eyes): Jog ring with rivets; Bandana: Master; Cloth scrap A / B (floating): Info; Lower scraps (floating): FX rows; Upper teeth: 16 hot-cue pads. Floating: Four cloth scraps (deck A, deck B, FX A, FX B), treasure-map browser, app buttons. Usability 2/5 (diagonal faders and scattered scraps).

## 06 Steampunk
Aged bone in brass goggles whose gear rims are the jog rings; each goggle shows low, mid and high bands as three stacked lanes. Brass trumpets for ears carry the effects and tempo, and the mixer is a separate riveted console with a vertebra stack for master and headphones. Control families: Goggles (eyes): Gear-ring jog; Forehead: Info; Cheekbones: Transport; Trumpet ears: FX rows; Nose: Mic. Floating: Brass console, ledger browser, tray with sampler and app buttons. Usability 3/5 (rivets are busy; three-band lanes are small).

## 07 Crystal Glass
A faceted crystal skull lit from inside: the eyes are glass orbs and the waveform is a beam of light passing through both orbs and the bridge. Knobs and pads are cut gems, the brow ridge carries the transport, and the mixer is a spine of crystal segments. Control families: Orbs (eyes): Jog ring with gem studs; Brow ridge: Play / cue / sync and key lock / quantise / slip / reverse; Forehead: Info; Temples: Tempo + range; Cheekbones: FX slots (3 columns). Floating: Crystal-shard browser, REC and app buttons tray. Usability 3/5 (translucency lowers label contrast on bright wallpapers).

## 08 Tiki Mask
A tall carved mask: the eyes are rings of coloured beads (the jog) and a single inlaid band runs through both with deck A growing upward and deck B downward from a shared centre line. The huge mouth is the mixer, framed by two rows of teeth. Control families: Eyes: Bead-ring jog; Forehead plaque: Info; Feather crown: 8 sampler pads; Nose bridge: Transport (vertical); Cheeks: Toggles. Floating: Totem-base browser, hanging FX planks, app buttons. Usability 3/5 (wood is dark but mouth mixer is far from eyes).

## 09 Wireframe Diagram
The skull is only an outline: eyes are jog rings with a thin line-drawn waveform (one continuous line crossing the bridge), teeth are pads, and the crossfader rides the jaw curve. Every other control lives on clean paper panels that float beside it, so the drawing stays calm and usability stays maximal. Control families: Eyes: Jog ring; Upper teeth: 16 hot-cue pads; Lower teeth: 16 sampler pads; Jaw: Crossfader on a curved rail; Deck panels A / B (floating): Info. Floating: Two deck panels, master/mic panel, app panel, browser. Usability 5/5 (high contrast, everything labelled, nothing hidden).

## 10 X-Ray Open Jaw (owner's request)
Owner's request: caricature-large eye sockets, the jaw dropped wide open and the full cervical spine on show. The sockets hold the whole deck read-out (title, big waveform, BPM, time, overview) inside a giant jog ring; the open mouth holds the mixer; seven vertebrae carry FX, master, faders, mic and app buttons, so only the browser floats. Control families: Giant sockets: Jog ring (464 px); Brow: Play / cue / sync; Temples: Tempo + range; Cheekbones: Pad modes; Upper teeth: 16 hot-cue pads. Floating: Only the track browser floats (X-ray film sheet with grip, slightly tilted) plus the status pill. Usability 4/5 (largest targets, everything on one surface).

## Recommendation: the three strongest
1. **10 X-Ray Open Jaw** - It is the owner request and the strongest performance skin: everything except the browser is on one surface, with the biggest waveforms and jogs, and it needs only features the other concepts also need.
2. **09 Wireframe Diagram** - The most usable by far and the cheapest engine-wise (outline plus panels); it proves the skull idea works even stripped to lines, and is a good first shipping skull.
3. **01 Anatomical Ivory** - The most literal answer to the brief: clear anatomy-to-control mapping, one continuous waveform through both eyes, and a shipping-quality look without exotic effects.

## Consolidated engine needs, ordered by how many concepts need them
1. Per-window transparency with shaped hit-testing (outline + cut-out holes such as nasal cavity and open mouth; clicks fall through transparent pixels) - 10 of 10 concepts
2. Multiple independent floating windows with remembered positions, optional snapping/docking to each other - 10 of 10 concepts
3. Drag-region / grip declaration per window (move the window by a skull part or frame grip, no title bar) - 10 of 10 concepts
4. Annular/round jog widget: ring hit-test, rotating position marker, progress arc, centre area that passes clicks to the waveform inside - 10 of 10 concepts
5. Waveform (and text) widgets clipped to a circle/polygon (eye socket, visor, lens) instead of a rectangle - 8 of 10 concepts
6. Per-widget shape outlines for buttons/pads (tooth, chamfer, gem, petal) with per-pixel hit testing - 8 of 10 concepts
7. Animations on containers (transform/opacity of a group including children): jaw drop/sway, eye pulse, vertebra bounce - 7 of 10 concepts
8. Style properties (glow, opacity, scale, rotate) bindable to level meters and beat phase (eye glow follows level, teeth light as VU) - 5 of 10 concepts
9. Waveform layouts: stacked lanes (3-band), mirrored shared-centreline halves, outline/line mode, glow beam - 5 of 10 concepts
10. Soft glow / blur and additive blend per widget - 3 of 10 concepts
11. Slider/fader travelling along a path (arc jaw rail, diagonal bone) with matching hit-testing - 2 of 10 concepts
12. Value-driven rotation (needle gauges bound to meters) - 1 of 10 concepts
13. Nearest-neighbour integer scaling and a bitmap font (no smoothing) for the pixel look - 1 of 10 concepts
14. Rotated widgets (fader caps and knob rows on a diagonal) - 1 of 10 concepts
