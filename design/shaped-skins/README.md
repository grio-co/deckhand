# DeckHand shaped skins: eleven non-skull concepts

Every mockup is the main player as one shaped window on a transparent canvas (`mockup-transparent.png`; `mockup.png` is the same over a wallpaper), with the browser, sampler and other modules as separate floating windows in the same theme and a grip motif. All art is original and generated by `NN-name/make.py` with the shared `lib.py` (standard library; rendered with headless Chrome).

## 01 Boombox
A ghetto-blaster whose two speaker cones are the platters: the rubber surround is the jog ring, the dust cap shows deck letter and BPM. A tape-style display in the middle carries both waveforms, the mixer sits between the cones, and the browser is a floating cassette. Silhouette: Wide rounded body with an arched carry handle (see-through gap) and a telescopic antenna. Floating: Cassette (browser) and tape-door (sampler, REC, app buttons) float below; status pill. Usability 4/5, buildability 4/5.

## 02 Octopus
The two eyes are the decks (jog ring around a read-out), a brow-plate carries both waveforms in stacked lanes, and the tentacles are the controls: the outer pair are tempo sliders, the next pair carry the EQ knobs strung along them, the third pair are curved channel faders, and the inner pair hold loop buttons like suckers. Silhouette: Mantle dome with two eyes and eight tapering tentacles curling downward. Floating: Clam-shell browser and a bubble cluster with the sampler. Usability 2/5, buildability 2/5.

## 03 Scarab (closed / open)
A scarab whose wing cases open to reveal the mixer: open, each elytra is a deck (jog, waveform, pads, loops, transport, tempo) and the abdomen between them is the mixer; closed, the shell shrinks to a compact two-deck player. Both states are drawn in the mockup; the browser is a rolling dung ball. Silhouette: Beetle seen from above with two huge elytra swung open, head, legs; closed form is a single oval. Floating: Closed compact beetle (alternate state of the main window) and a circular dung-ball browser. Usability 3/5, buildability 3/5.

## 04 Reel-to-Reel
Each deck is a reel: the hub is the jog and read-out, the rim carries a position arc, and the visible tape pack shrinks as the track plays. The tower between the reels is the tape head with both waveforms as two tape runs; the plinth holds the mixer and per-deck controls. Silhouette: Two huge reels flanking a head tower over a plinth with feet. Floating: Tape-box browser and a stadium-shaped tape-loop cartridge. Usability 4/5, buildability 4/5.

## 05 Satellite
A satellite: the two solar wings are the decks (jog, pads, loops, transport, tempo) and the hub between them is the mixer with the shared waveform display on top. A detached solar array is the browser and a probe pod holds the sampler. Silhouette: Central hub with antenna and two chamfered solar wings on truss arms. Floating: Solar-array browser on hinge arms and a rounded probe pod. Usability 4/5, buildability 5/5.

## 06 Spectacles
Twin round lenses are the platters (rim = jog, glass = waveform and read-out) and the tall bridge between them is the mixer. Bifocal tabs below each lens hold pads, loops and transport; the temple arms are vertical tempo sliders. Silhouette: Two round lenses joined by a tall bridge plate, vertical temple arms and bifocal tabs. Floating: Glasses case (browser) and a wavy-edged cloth tray. Usability 3/5, buildability 4/5.

## 07 Orrery
A brass orrery: the sun is the master with an arc meter, two planets are the decks, hot-cue pads ride the outer ring turned to follow it, EQ knobs orbit above each planet and the channel faders and tempo sliders are arcs. The crossfader is the lower orbit. Silhouette: A single big brass ring (the only circular main window of the set). Floating: Scroll browser with brass rollers and a hexagonal sampler tile. Usability 3/5, buildability 2/5.

## 08 Lighthouse (tall and narrow)
A lighthouse designed to live at a screen edge: the lantern is the master with a beacon-bulb arc meter, then deck A, the mixer, deck B and a door at the base, stacked top to bottom. A sailing boat floats beside it as the browser with the sampler on its sail. Silhouette: Tall narrow tower (about 4.6 wide to 1 high) for the screen edge, with a gallery and a lantern. Floating: A boat (browser hull, sampler on the sail) and a status pill. Usability 3/5, buildability 4/5.

## 09 Pebble (compact pocket mode)
An extremely compact two-deck player: two mini platters with play inside, a two-lane waveform, BPMs, crossfader and cue / sync, all at 1x with 28 px minimum targets. Everything else (hot cues, loops, EQ and faders, browser) lives in small docks that appear on demand beside it. Silhouette: A smooth pebble with a thumb dent, small enough for a screen corner. Floating: Four docks: hot-cue pill, loop pill, mix dock and track picker. Usability 3/5, buildability 5/5.

## 10 Mushroom Cluster
Two big toadstools are the decks: the cap face holds the platter, title and waveform, and the gills underneath fan out as the eight hot-cue pads. Stems carry transport, loops and tempo; the small purple mushroom in between is the mixer, and the crossfader runs along the roots. Silhouette: Two big caps with a small purple cap between them, flared stems, mossy ground. Floating: A bracket-fungus shelf (browser) and a spore-bubble cluster (sampler). Usability 2/5, buildability 2/5.

## 11 Predator Owl
A menacing owl mid-strike: heavy angled brow slabs carry the deck read-outs, two enormous glowing eyes are the decks (iris = jog ring, pupil = centre, one waveform band running through both eyes), the hooked beak holds the master knob, the chest is the mixer, the spread wings carry rows of feather-shaped buttons, and each foot is a set of four talon-toes for load, sync, play and cue. Silhouette: Head-on bird of prey with ear tufts, spread wings with fanned primaries, and talons reaching down. Floating: Perch branch (browser), shed feather (effects) and a pellet (sampler); status pill. Usability 3/5, buildability 2/5.

## Most buildable with the current engine
05 Satellite (5/5), 09 Pebble (compact pocket mode) (5/5), 01 Boombox (4/5), 04 Reel-to-Reel (4/5) - mostly chamfered rectangles, rings and standard widgets; 07, 02 and 10 depend on path sliders and rotated widgets.

## Consolidated engine needs, by how many concepts need them
1. Per-window transparency with shaped hit-testing (alpha-based hit = alpha, cut-out holes such as handle gaps and reel spokes; docs/SKINS.md has the outline polygon, limit 256 points, but the shell still shows a rectangle) - 11 of 11
2. Multiple independent floating windows with remembered positions, optional snapping/docking to each other (browser, sampler, effects etc. are separate skin windows) - 11 of 11
3. Drag-region declaration per window (move by the body or the grip motif; no title bar) - 11 of 11
4. Annular/round jog widget: ring hit-test, rotating position marker, progress arc, centre area that passes clicks to the waveform or text inside - 11 of 11
5. Waveform, text and widget clipping to a circle or polygon (eye, lens, porthole) instead of a rectangle - 9 of 11
6. Sliders and knob chains that travel along a bezier/arc path with matching hit-testing (tentacles, orbits, roots, crossfader smile) - 4 of 11
7. Rotated widgets (pads and buttons following a ring or a fan) - 3 of 11
8. Arc-shaped segmented meters bound to a level (beacon bulb, sun) - 2 of 11
9. Compact/expanded window modes with remembered positions (switch between a tiny shell and the full skin, docks that appear on demand) - 2 of 11
10. Two-state shell (closed/open) with animated transform of grouped widgets and swapping of window layouts - 1 of 11
11. Screen-edge docking and auto-hide for a tall narrow window - 1 of 11
